Small retailers lose 15-20% of inventory value annually due to poor tracking, leading to stockouts (lost sales) and overstock (waste/markdowns).
Trigger Situation
Running out of a key product unexpectedly or discovering expired inventory during a rush period
Underlying Goal
Know exactly what to order and when, without spending hours on spreadsheets

E3 (Industry reports) + E4 (Hypothesis)

💀 Pre-Mortem Analysis
5 failure scenarios
Price Sensitivity Kills Adoption 40% probability
Target customers are extremely price-sensitive. $79/mo may be too high for businesses with thin margins. Competitors offering free tiers capture market.
Kill criteria: If landing page test shows less than 2% conversion at $79
Spreadsheet Inertia Too Strong 35% probability
Users have built workflows around spreadsheets over years. Switching cost (learning, data migration, habit change) exceeds perceived benefit.
Kill criteria: If 8+ of 10 interviewees say they would not switch even with proven ROI
⚖ Legal & Regulatory Risks
Grounded
CCPA Compliance (California)
Required if serving California retailers. 3-6 months implementation, ~$30K cost. Not a blocker.
SOC 2 Certification
Recommended for enterprise sales. 6-9 months, $50-100K. Can defer to Year 2.
