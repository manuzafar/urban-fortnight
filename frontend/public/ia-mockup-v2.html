Core Problem Statement Unvalidated
Small retailers lose 15-20% of inventory value annually due to poor tracking, leading to stockouts (lost sales) and overstock (waste/markdowns). This costs the average store $15,000-$30,000 per year.
Trigger Situation
Running out of a key product unexpectedly or discovering expired inventory during a rush period
Underlying Goal
Know exactly what to order and when, without spending hours on spreadsheets
Success Definition
Never run out of top sellers, reduce waste by 50%, save 5+ hours per week
