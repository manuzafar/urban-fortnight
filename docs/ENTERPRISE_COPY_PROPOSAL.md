## Research Summary

### Enterprise Product Discovery Pain Points

Based on research from [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/technology-media-and-telecommunications/our-insights/how-generative-ai-could-accelerate-software-product-time-to-market), [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/innovation-strategy), [Pendo](https://www.pendo.io/pendo-blog/why-the-product-discovery-process-is-broken-and-how-to-fix-it/), and [Productboard](https://www.productboard.com/blog/step-by-step-framework-for-better-product-discovery/):

| Pain Point | Data |
|------------|------|
| Knowledge bottlenecks | 70% of R&D time spent on documentation, not innovation |
| Information searching | Knowledge workers spend 20% of time just finding information |
| Product failure rate | 80% of software features are rarely or never used |
| Wasted R&D | $29.5B wasted annually on failed products |
| Discovery timeline | Weeks to months for stakeholder alignment |
| Siloed decisions | No single owner of idea-to-delivery process |

### Enterprise Buyer Priorities (2025)

From [CIO.com](https://www.cio.com/article/3801023/10-top-priorities-for-cios-in-2025.html), [McKinsey CTO Priorities](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/retail/our-insights/eight-tech-forward-imperatives-for-consumer-ctos-in-2025), and [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/tmt/library/tech-cio-priorities.html):

1. **AI that delivers ROI** — Not experiments, actual business value
2. **Speed to market** — Agility is no longer optional
3. **Cost discipline** — Prove ROI, make trade-offs
4. **Reducing tech debt** — Modernize without disrupting
5. **Governance & trust** — AI needs guardrails

### Agentic AI Context (2025)

From [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/thought-leadership/institute-business-value/en-us/report/agentic-ai-operating-model), [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/topics/technology-management/tech-trends/2026/agentic-ai-strategy.html), and [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027):

- 79% of organizations have some agentic AI adoption
- 89% of CIOs consider agent-based AI a strategic priority
- Average ROI of 171% (192% in US enterprises)
- **BUT**: 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be cancelled due to unclear value
- "Agent washing" is rampant — most vendors aren't real

**Key insight**: Enterprises want agentic AI but are skeptical. They need **proven value**, not hype.

## Target Persona Analysis

### Primary: VP of Product / Chief Product Officer

**Their world:**
- Responsible for product strategy and portfolio
- Measured on speed-to-market, product-market fit, revenue impact
- Frustrated by slow discovery cycles that delay launches
- Under pressure to "do more with less" while competitors move faster

**What they need to hear:**
- "Compress discovery without cutting corners"
- "Get stakeholder alignment before the first meeting"
- "Ship decisions, not slide decks"

### Secondary: CTO / VP Engineering

**Their world:**
- Responsible for technical feasibility and architecture
- Frustrated when handed PRDs that are technically naive
- Wants to be involved earlier but doesn't have time
- Skeptical of AI-generated content (has seen hallucinations)

**What they need to hear:**
- "Technical architecture validated against your actual constraints"
- "Legal and regulatory review built in, not bolted on"
- "Critique agent catches contradictions before engineers do"

### Tertiary: Chief Innovation Officer / Strategy Lead

**Their world:**
- Responsible for new business lines and ventures
- Evaluating dozens of ideas with limited resources
- Needs fast, structured evaluation to prioritize bets
- Reports to board on innovation pipeline health

**What they need to hear:**
- "Evaluate more ideas with the same resources"
- "Decision-ready packs for investment committees"
- "From concept to business case in a single session"

## Positioning Strategy

### Current Positioning (Too Generic)
> "The inception layer for modern product teams"

**Problem**: Doesn't speak to enterprise pain. "Modern product teams" sounds like startups. "Inception layer" is abstract.

### Recommended Positioning (Enterprise-Focused)

**Option A: Speed + Governance**
> "From concept to decision-ready — in minutes, not months"

**Option B: Agentic AI That Actually Works**
> "The AI product discovery platform that enterprises trust"

**Option C: Anti-Bottleneck**
> "Kill the discovery bottleneck. Ship what matters."

**Option D: Portfolio Thinking**
> "Evaluate 10x more ideas. Launch the right ones."

### My Recommendation: Option A + Enterprise Trust

**Headline:**
> **From concept to decision-ready**
> **in minutes, not months.**

**Why this works:**
- "Concept to decision-ready" is the full journey (not just "idea to pack")
- "Minutes, not months" is a bold, specific claim that challenges status quo
- Implies speed AND completeness (decision-ready = thorough)
- Enterprise buyers understand "decision-ready" (board-ready, investment-committee-ready)

## Tone Guidelines for Enterprise

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use "decision-ready" — they understand it | Use "ship faster" — sounds like startup culture |
| Say "stakeholder alignment" — it's their pain | Say "get stuff done" — too casual |
| Reference "governance" and "audit-ready" | Ignore compliance concerns |
| Acknowledge AI skepticism ("no hallucinations") | Overpromise AI capabilities |
| Use specific numbers (12 minutes, 7 deliverables) | Use vague claims ("super fast") |
| Show the architecture (swarms, agents) | Hide how it works |
| Mention cross-validation and critique | Position as magic black box |
