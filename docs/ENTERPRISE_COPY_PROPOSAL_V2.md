## Research Context

### The Enterprise Problem (2025)

From [McKinsey State of AI 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai) and [Deloitte Enterprise AI Report](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/what-we-do/capabilities/applied-artificial-intelligence/content/state-of-ai-in-the-enterprise.html):

- **39%** of enterprises see any EBIT impact from AI
- **42%** of AI projects were abandoned in 2025 (up from 17%)
- **66%** report productivity gains, but only **20%** report revenue growth
- The gap: AI tools exist, but **outcomes don't follow**

From [ISG PLM Buyers Guide 2025](https://research.isg-one.com/buyers-guide/operations_and_supply_chain/product_lifecycle_management/2025):

- PLM market: $34.7B in 2024 → $59.5B by 2030
- Leading enterprises compress cycles by **50%** with connected systems
- The winners: Those who connect **ideation → validation → delivery** in one system

**The insight:**
> Enterprises don't need another AI tool. They need AI that connects to their context and accelerates their outcomes.
