# World-Class Product Discovery Framework

*Synthesized from 8 legendary product leaders on Lenny's Podcast*

---

## Executive Summary

This framework transforms LifeCycle from "AI does everything" to "AI guides the PM through world-class product discovery." It synthesizes battle-tested wisdom from:

- **Teresa Torres** (Opportunity Solution Tree, Continuous Discovery)
- **Bob Moesta** (Jobs to Be Done, 4 Forces Model)
- **Marty Cagan** (Empowered Teams, Value + Viability)
- **Uri Levine** (Fall in Love with the Problem, PMF = Retention)
- **Dalton Caldwell** (YC: Just Don't Die, Tarpit Ideas)
- **Shreyas Doshi** (Pre-mortems, LNO Framework, High Agency)
- **Gibson Biddle** (DHM Model: Delight/Hard-to-copy/Margin)
- **Melissa Perri** (Strategy Deployment, Product vs Feature Teams)

---

## Core Philosophy Shift

### FROM: "AI generates everything for you"
### TO: "AI guides you through the thinking process"

| Old Approach | New Approach |
|--------------|--------------|
| AI generates customer research | AI teaches you HOW to interview customers |
| AI identifies pain points | AI guides you to DISCOVER struggling moments |
| AI creates strategy | AI walks you through THINKING frameworks |
| Passive PM receives outputs | Active PM builds discovery muscle |

## Phase 1: PROBLEM LOVE

### Source: Uri Levine (Waze founder, 2 unicorns)

> "The only metric for product-market fit is retention. If you create value, they come back."

### Guided Experience

**AI Prompt to PM:**
```
Before we dive into building anything, let's ensure you're solving
a problem worth solving.

Uri Levine built two $1B companies by following one rule:
"Fall in love with the problem, not the solution."

Let me guide you through his framework:

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
   What's the problem you're solving?
   [Text input with AI coaching]

2. PROBLEM VALIDATION
   Can you name 5 people who have this problem RIGHT NOW?
   Not "people might" - but people you KNOW are struggling?

   [ ] Person 1: _____________ (their struggling moment)
   [ ] Person 2: _____________
   [ ] Person 3: _____________
   [ ] Person 4: _____________
   [ ] Person 5: _____________

3. FREQUENCY CHECK (Uri's litmus test)
   How often do people encounter this problem?

   [ ] Daily - "This could be huge"
   [ ] Weekly - "Strong potential"
   [ ] Monthly - "Needs 10X solution"
   [ ] Rarely - "Red flag: harder to build habit"

4. CURRENT ALTERNATIVES
   What do people do TODAY to solve this?
   (If nothing exists, that's a warning sign - Uri Levine)
```

### AI Coaching Interjections

**If PM provides vague problem:**
```
"I notice your problem statement is broad. Uri Levine says:
'A problem that is everyone's problem is no one's problem.'

Can you narrow this to a specific moment when someone struggles?

Example: Instead of 'People need better productivity tools'
Try: 'Product managers waste 3+ hours weekly searching for
competitor pricing data scattered across 12 different sources'"
```

**If PM can't name 5 people:**
```
"You couldn't name 5 people with this problem. This is actually
a CRITICAL signal.

Dalton Caldwell (YC) says: 'Talk to customers BEFORE writing code.
If you can't find people with the problem, the problem might not
be real enough.'

Option A: Go find 5 people first (recommend)
Option B: Proceed with hypothesis (higher risk)
```

## Phase 2: CUSTOMER TRUTH

### Source: Teresa Torres (Continuous Discovery Habits)

> "Interview weekly. Collect stories, not answers. The opportunity is an unmet need, pain point, or desire."

### The Story-First Interview Framework

**AI Guide:**
```
Teresa Torres transformed product discovery by teaching one principle:
"Don't ask what people want. Collect stories of what they actually do."

I'll help you prepare for customer interviews that ACTUALLY work.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
THE GOLDEN QUESTION (Never ask what they want)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

❌ BAD: "What features would you like?"
❌ BAD: "Would you use a product that...?"
❌ BAD: "What's your biggest pain point?"

✅ GOOD: "Tell me about the last time you [struggled with X]..."
✅ GOOD: "Walk me through what happened..."
✅ GOOD: "What did you do next?"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

**Interview Script Generator:**
```
Based on your problem: [X]

Here's your interview guide:

OPENING (2 min)
"Thanks for chatting. I'm exploring [problem space]. I'd love to hear
about your experience - there's no right or wrong answers."

STORY COLLECTION (15 min)
1. "Tell me about the last time you [dealt with problem]..."
2. "Walk me through what happened step by step..."
3. "What did you try first?" → "Then what?" → "Then what?"
4. "What was frustrating about that?"
5. "How did it end up?"

CONTEXT MINING (10 min)
6. "What were you trying to accomplish?"
7. "Why was that important to you at that moment?"
8. "What would 'good' have looked like?"

CURRENT BEHAVIOR (5 min)
9. "What do you use today to handle this?"
10. "What's annoying about that?"
11. "If you could wave a magic wand..."

CLOSE (3 min)
"This was incredibly helpful. Anyone else you know who struggles with this?"
```

## Phase 3: OPPORTUNITY MAPPING

### Source: Bob Moesta (Jobs to Be Done), Teresa Torres (OST)

> "People don't buy products. They hire products to make progress." - Bob Moesta

### The 4 Forces Model

**AI Coaching:**
```
Bob Moesta discovered that every decision to change (buy, adopt, switch)
involves 4 forces:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    FORCES OF PROGRESS                           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                 │
│    FORCES PUSHING FOR CHANGE        FORCES BLOCKING CHANGE      │
│    ─────────────────────────        ─────────────────────────   │
│                                                                 │
│    F1: PUSH                         F3: ANXIETY                 │
│    "The problem with              "What if the new              │
│    current situation"              thing doesn't work?"         │
│         ↓                                ↑                      │
│    [████████████]  ─────────────→  [████████]                   │
│                                                                 │
│    F2: PULL                         F4: HABIT                   │
│    "The attraction of             "The comfort of               │
│    the new solution"               what I know"                 │
│         ↓                                ↑                      │
│    [██████████████] ─────────────→ [██████████]                 │
│                                                                 │
│    CHANGE HAPPENS WHEN:                                         │
│    F1 + F2  >  F3 + F4                                         │
│                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Let's map YOUR customer's 4 forces from their stories:
```

**Force Mapping Exercise:**
```
Based on interviews, identify:

F1: PUSH (What's broken today?)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
From interviews, what "struggling moments" keep coming up?

• [Story snippet]: "I spent 4 hours..."
  → Push force: Time wasted on manual research

• [Story snippet]: "My boss asked and I didn't know..."
  → Push force: Looking uninformed to stakeholders

F2: PULL (What's attractive about new?)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What outcomes do customers describe wanting?

• "I wish I could just have it ready..."
  → Pull: Instant answers, look prepared

F3: ANXIETY (What scares them about switching?)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What concerns emerged in interviews?

• "I don't trust AI to get the details right..."
  → Anxiety: Accuracy concerns

F4: HABIT (What's comfortable about status quo?)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What keeps them from changing?

• "I've always done it this way..."
  → Habit: Familiar process, known effort
```

## Phase 4: SOLUTION DESIGN

### Source: Gibson Biddle (Netflix), Marty Cagan

> "Delight customers in hard-to-copy, margin-enhancing ways" - Gibson Biddle

### The DHM Framework

**AI Coaching:**
```
Gibson Biddle led product at Netflix. His simple framework:

D - DELIGHT
    "Does this truly delight customers? Not satisfy. Delight."

H - HARD TO COPY
    "Can competitors replicate this tomorrow?"

M - MARGIN ENHANCING
    "Does this build a sustainable business?"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

For each solution idea, let's score it:

SOLUTION: [Your idea]

D - DELIGHT SCORE (1-10)
    How much better is this than alternatives?
    (Gibson says: "10X better or don't bother")

    Score: [  ]
    Evidence: _______________

H - HARD TO COPY SCORE (1-10)
    Network effects? Unique data? Brand? Economies of scale?

    Score: [  ]
    Moat type: [ ] Network effects
               [ ] Data advantage
               [ ] Brand/trust
               [ ] Economies of scale
               [ ] Switching costs
               [ ] Technology

M - MARGIN SCORE (1-10)
    Will this improve retention? Reduce CAC? Enable pricing power?

    Score: [  ]
    Impact: _______________

TOTAL DHM: [  ] / 30

Gibson's Rule: "If total < 20, reconsider. If < 15, reject."
```

                         ↑
  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ RUNG 4: SMOKE TEST                                    │
  │ "Will they put $ down?"                               │
  │ Method: Landing page with pricing, measure conversion │
  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                         ↑
  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ RUNG 3: PROTOTYPE TEST                                │

  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

### LNO Task Prioritization (Shreyas Doshi)

**AI Guide:**
```
Shreyas Doshi's LNO Framework helps you focus:

L - LEVERAGE TASKS (10X return)
    "These are the scary important things you're avoiding"

N - NEUTRAL TASKS (1X return)
    "Standard work that needs doing"

O - OVERHEAD TASKS (<1X return)
    "Necessary but low impact"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

For your discovery work:

L TASKS (Do these first, do them well):
• Customer interviews
• Synthesizing insights into opportunities
• Strategic decisions about direction
• Alignment conversations with stakeholders

N TASKS (Do efficiently):
• Documenting research findings
• Creating interview schedules
• Reviewing competitor products

O TASKS (Minimize time):
• Formatting documents
• Organizing files
• Administrative tasks

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

"The cave you fear contains the treasure you seek."
Your L tasks are probably the ones you're avoiding.

What L task have you been putting off?
```
